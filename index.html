Cape Disappointment

Lewis & Clark Interpretive Center

Park here. Really cool museum about the area and the expedition, and the balcony has amazing views.

15-min walk + a climb

Cape Disappointment Lighthouse

Walk out from the L&C center — about 15 min, with a fairly good climb for part of it. Oldest lighthouse on the West Coast, looking out over the Columbia bar, the “Graveyard of the Pacific” (literally).

short detour off the trail

Deadman's Cove

Off the lighthouse trail — a quiet, driftwood-strewn cove. Worth the scramble down and the climb back out.

6 min drive

North Head Lighthouse

Yes, worth doing even though you JUST did a lighthouse. Easier walk, no real climb. One of the best sunset spots on the peninsula.

15 min up the coast

Cranberry Museum

More gift shop than museum, but you can walk around a working cranberry bog — pretty interesting to see how it all works.

5 min drive

Marsh's Free Museum, Long Beach

Long Beach has a nice boardwalk and shops; Marsh's is the must-do — crazy stuff crammed into every corner, including “Jake the Alligator Man.” It's free, it's weird, go in.

to stay the night

Pearl Inn Bed & Breakfast

A great place to stay for a night in the area.

Astoria

Astoria Column

Climb the spiral staircase for views over the Columbia mouth, the bridge to Washington, and — on a clear day — Mt. Rainier and Mt. St. Helens. Deer wander the grounds. Buy a wooden glider from the gift shop before you climb — you won't want the stairs twice.

5 min down the hill

Columbia River Maritime Museum

Genuinely amazing museum — shipwrecks, the Coast Guard, and the story of the Columbia River bar, a.k.a. the “Graveyard of the Pacific.”

5 min walk along the river

Riverwalk & Frite & Scoop

The quick visit: stroll the Astoria Riverwalk along the water and grab ice cream at Frite & Scoop.

10 min walk

Pier 39 & Hanthorn Cannery Museum

A cool restored 1875 cannery — shops and a small hotel now. If it's quiet you can drive right out onto the pier on a slightly rickety bridge (fun). Free cannery museum inside — quick and genuinely interesting. Sea lions sometimes hang out here.

Fort Stevens

Wreck of the Peter Iredale

An old shipwreck half-buried in the sand — a 1906 schooner, just the rusted ribs left. Super cool to see. You can drive right on the beach in parts (you could last time, at least — worth checking it's still allowed).

7 min away

Battery Russell

Really cool WWII fortification — multi-level and fully open to explore. Dark rooms, a bit creepy, great for hide-and-seek. The only military base in the continental U.S. that got fired on by a foreign enemy in WWII (a Japanese sub, 1942).

Cannon Beach45 min south

Cannon Beach & Ecola

Cannon Beach & Haystack Rock

The Goonies car-race beach. Walk the sand, gawk at Haystack Rock, poke around the cool shops downtown. One of the most photographed spots on the coast — for a reason.

10 min — and the drive alone is worth it

Indian Beach, Ecola State Park

Walk down to the beach and head for the rocky/boulder areas — great tide pools if you time it right. Way better than Cannon Beach for this (no volunteer monitors shooing you along, far fewer people). This is the “La Push” beach from Twilight.

Tillamookabout an hour south — its own day

Tillamook

Tillamook Creamery

The famous cheese factory. Self-guided tour, cheese tastings, and the famously long ice-cream line (worth it). But the real move: the grilled cheese sandwiches in the cafe — amazing.
